\section{Further figures}
\label{sec:figures} We place the remaining panels in this supplement.
Figure~\ref{fig:layout} is the CON frame beside the fields a version 1 reader keeps.
Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline} is the three-step parse.
Figure~\ref{fig:matrix} marks which format pairs keep a displacement.
Figure~\ref{fig:threads} is the committed thread sweep.
Figure~\ref{fig:layers} is the layer throughput.
Figure~\ref{fig:roofline} places that throughput on the stored sweep.
Figure~\ref{fig:dbin} and Figure~\ref{fig:dbbw} are the ingest rate and the bandwidth.
Figure~\ref{fig:dbq} is the query time.
Figure~\ref{fig:dbdisk} is the allocated bytes.
Figure~\ref{fig:dbdedup} compares stored frames with unique hashes.
Figure~\ref{fig:rdiff} is the displacement after a round trip and after six decimals.
Figure~\ref{fig:fidelity} is the field matrix.
Figure~\ref{fig:anatomy} is the byte layout of one frame.
Figure~\ref{fig:velocity} is the displacement that follows a mislabeled velocity block.
Figure~\ref{fig:errors} is the class of each illegal file.
Figure~\ref{fig:dedup} is the campaign hash graph.
Figure~\ref{fig:mask} is the held path and the frozen path.
Figure~\ref{fig:multiples} is the energy of the three resumed bands.
Figure~\ref{fig:mpistrong} and Figure~\ref{fig:mpiweak} are the committed MPI sweeps.
