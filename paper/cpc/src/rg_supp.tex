\section{Reserved keys and sections}
\label{sec:keys}
Table~\ref{tab:keys} is the header keys the reader recognises.
A key that is not in the table is kept on a rewrite and is not checked.
Table~\ref{tab:sections} is the sections reserved at minor 1.
Each data row carries its values, then the mask and the identifier.
The value count is the number of numeric fields before those two.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\caption{Reserved keys of the JSON header.}
\label{tab:keys}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.34\linewidth}p{0.16\linewidth}p{0.42\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Key & Type & Role \\
\midrule
\texttt{con\_spec\_version} & integer & Major version \\
\texttt{con\_spec\_minor} & integer & Minor version, 0 if absent \\
\texttt{units} & object & Length and energy from major 3; time from minor 1 when velocities are present \\
\texttt{pbc} & 3 booleans & Periodic flags. Absent means periodic in all three directions \\
\texttt{lattice\_vectors} & $3\times 3$ & Cell, in place of lines 3 and 4 \\
\texttt{sections} & strings & Sections after the coordinates, in order \\
\texttt{section\_arity} & object & Value count of a section skipped when the minor is later \\
\texttt{validate} & boolean & Extra checks on the cell and the rows \\
\texttt{generator} & string & Tool that wrote the frame \\
\texttt{energy} & number & Frame energy \\
\texttt{time} & number & Simulation time \\
\texttt{timestep} & number & Integration step \\
\texttt{fmax} & number & Maximum force \\
\texttt{convergence\_fmax} & number & Force threshold \\
\texttt{convergence\_energy} & number & Energy threshold \\
\texttt{converged} & boolean & Writer's convergence flag \\
\texttt{frame\_index} & integer & Index in a trajectory \\
\texttt{neb\_bead} & integer & Image index \\
\texttt{neb\_band} & integer & Band index \\
\texttt{potential} & object & Force-field descriptor \\
\texttt{bonds} & array & Pairs of row indices \\
\texttt{storage\_dtypes} & object & In-memory element types \\
\texttt{chemfiles\_atom\_names} & array & Sidecar from a chemfiles import \\
\texttt{chemfiles\_atom\_types} & array & Sidecar from a chemfiles import \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
