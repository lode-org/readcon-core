On eight threads the retimed in-memory reads are at least 0.8 efficient (Table~\ref{tab:r6-read}).
The million-atom CON read is \effReadConOneE, the alloy extended XYZ read is \effReadAlloy, the molecular-dynamics extended XYZ read is \effReadExtxyzMd, and the same-format million-atom extended XYZ read is \effReadExtxyzSame.
The \num{100000}-frame CON read is \effReadConHundredK.
The convel read, \effReadConvel, and the copper extended XYZ read, \effReadExtxyzCu, were not part of that retime.

Weak scaling is at least 0.87 at eight threads and at least 0.92 at eight ranks.

The writes are not final.
At eight threads the disk-write efficiency is \perfEightDiskWrite and the in-memory write efficiency is \perfEightMemWrite.
Those two stay placeholders while the eight-thread write is still being fixed.
