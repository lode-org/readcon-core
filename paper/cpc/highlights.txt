CON 3.1 is a versioned text grammar for saddle-search checkpoint files.
Each coordinate row carries a per-component mask and an atom identifier.
One Rust core serves C, C++, Python, Julia and Fortran, with DLPack views.
Two corpora of legal and illegal files test any reader against the specification.
Read times are measured against ASE, chemfiles, h5py and native trajectory readers.
