readcon-core reads and writes CON 3.1, a versioned grammar for atomistic checkpoints.
Every frame keeps atom ids, per-axis masks, masses and a declared velocity time unit.
One Rust core serves C, C++, Fortran, Julia and Python; eOn reads CON through it.
readcon-db stores CON corpora in LMDB; readcon-ops matches frames by atom identity.
Atom identifiers keep NEB, dimer and AKMC images paired across restarts and codes.
