CON 4 is a versioned geometry grammar with cell, periodicity, units and identifiers.
Each coordinate row carries a per-component mask and an atom identifier.
One Rust core serves C, C++, Python, Julia and Fortran, with DLPack and metatensor.
readcon-db, readcon-ops, minimage and linkcell share one cell convention.
Two corpora of legal and illegal files test any reader against the specification.
