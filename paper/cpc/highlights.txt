CON 3.1 is a versioned grammar for the CON geometry format.
The format defines per-component masks, atom identifiers and velocity time units.
Two corpora of legal and illegal files decide what a conforming reader accepts.
A climbing-image band resumed from CON 3.1 repeats the uninterrupted run bit for bit.
Read times are measured against ASE, chemfiles, h5py and native trajectory readers.
