Fresh-file 8-rank CON write, 2000 frames per rank, /dev/shm, taskset 0-7.
Each sample unlinks the published path first. The library creates a new
temporary file, set_len, stores, then renames. No scratch inode.

Store comparison, 4 samples, median of the two central samples:

  pwrite          t1 0.0410  t8 0.0868  eff 0.473
  MAP_POPULATE    t1 0.0429  t8 0.0696  eff 0.616
  fallocate+mmap  t1 0.0424  t8 0.0652  eff 0.650
  mmap copy       t1 0.0426  t8 0.0587  eff 0.726

The shipped store is the mmap copy on tmpfs and pwrite off tmpfs.
pwrite, MAP_POPULATE, and a per-rank fallocate were all slower.

Campaign result with that store: mpi write con t1 0.0424 t8 0.0578 eff 0.734.
mpi write convel t1 0.0597 t8 0.0843 eff 0.709.

GNU time -v of mpirun, 4 samples plus warmup, whole tree:
  1 rank   minor faults 23561   wall 0.59 s
  8 ranks  minor faults 173934  wall 0.81 s

strace -c of the writer ranks does not show that time in write or pwrite.
The copy into the shared mapping faults in user space, and those faults
take the file's mapping lock, so eight ranks do not fault in parallel.
perf is not available for this kernel (6.12.113+); strace and time are the record.
