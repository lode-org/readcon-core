Wall time on one machine, from the stopped run's sample times.
1k at n=12 is about 10 minutes.
100k at n=12 is about 30 minutes.
1M except the mmCIF reader, at n=6, is about 45 minutes.
1M mmCIF, at n=6, warm and cold, is about 4.3 hours.
Each finished mmCIF sample was about 1300 seconds, and the write is not timed.
traj, 1000 atoms and 8 frames, at n=12, is about 45 minutes.
The extra rows, MDCompress on XTC and chemfiles DCD at fc1e930, are about 25 minutes.
asv on that same 8-frame trajectory, at n=12, is about 30 minutes.
One machine, one after another, is about 7.2 hours.
In parallel the mmCIF shard sets the wait, about 4.3 hours.
The other shards finish within an hour.

Each shard writes its own directory. Same tip, same sample count for every tool at that size.
